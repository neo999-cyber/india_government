   The Rule (Extract C.1): too-early is strictly defined as: "the measure is in force but

   ELAPSED TIME AND THE EVIDENCE TIME ACCUMULATES."
   The Violation:
         L-0205 (India-EU agreement): Scored too-early . The claim text explicitly notes
         the agreement is "concluded but not in force" and that "no date exists for either
         [signature or entry into force]".
         MD + 1
         L-0188 (US 6 February trade framework): Scored too-early . The claim text notes
         the framework contains "an interim agreement not concluded since".
         MD + 1
   Impact: Assigning too-early to measures that are not in force breaks the core
   requirement of Rule C.1. Measures not in force have an obstacle of executive/legislative
   action, not elapsed time.
