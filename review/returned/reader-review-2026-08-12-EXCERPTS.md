## WHAT MAKES THIS REVIEW DIFFERENT FROM THE FIRST TWO

### 2 — the landing page shifts the site's claim

The reviewer read the intended claim correctly first — *what can responsibly be known about how India

> "By the bottom of the landing page, I expected an audit of the state's statistical behaviour at
> least as much as a record of India's substantive change."

Cause named in the brief: the discontinued school-staffing card said **"is what the rest of this site
is about."**

### 3 — the internal register on public surfaces

Three examples:

> "T4 BY THE SCHEMA'S LETTER" — reads as the author arguing with the site's own classification.
> "VALUE-AND-NOTE RECONCILED 2026-08-03" — repository archaeology inside "Why this verdict".
> "A low figure is not a fault — a survey run every five years covers exactly as it was designed to"
> — lectures an imagined reader.

The brief adds two constraints: a correction's withdrawn wording stays visible (rule 3), so **move
the trail rather than removing it**; and the absence-vocabulary emphasis stays — *"DISCONTINUED, NOT
MERELY UNRETRIEVED"* is that rule working and **the reviewer misattributed it.**

### 4 — the vocabulary overlaps before the site explains it differs

Kashmir is where it breaks: *"0 series," "Indicators 30,"* a charted series, and *"thirty series are
read through the Kashmir lens"* on one page. The reviewer worked the ontology out and says plainly it
is a structural problem rather than unfamiliarity.

### 5 — a taxonomy for the series findings

> the successful sentences reveal a comparator, a measurement event, or the stakes; the unsuccessful
> ones describe the chart's direction or explain the record's internal filing state.

Three named failures:

> "These are the figures as they stood before that" — every word requires the preceding prose.
> "The row MHA labels an estimate — a different row of the same table" — reads as a fragment of a
> data-cleaning discussion.

and the security-force series, where **the weak sentence is the finding and the strong one is the
caveat**, so the finding narrates the slope while the caveat carries the epistemic point.
