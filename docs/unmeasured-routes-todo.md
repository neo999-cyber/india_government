### 13. `L-0107` · `unmeasured[2]` · **not-published**
`data/ledger/education.json` — Contract teaching: collected for every teacher, published for none

**what:** The trained share of contract teachers after 2015-16

**why:** The predecessor system published it separately at 54 to 67 per cent against 79 to 82 for regular teachers - a 15-to-25-point gap. The successor collects both the qualification field and the appointment field and publishes neither cross-tab, and the independent computation could not produce it for 2021-22 from the public release. Contract teaching and untrained teaching were the same problem, and the state stopped being able to say so.
