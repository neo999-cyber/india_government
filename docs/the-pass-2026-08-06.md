# The pass of 6 August 2026 — why the successes changed

On 6 August 2026 this instrument changed the standard a record must meet before it says a measure
worked, and applied the new standard to every record at once. **Nine records said *worked* before
the pass. One does after it.** Thirty-three citations changed tier and three records left the ledger.

This is the whole of it, in one list. Nothing was moved for a reason not stated here.

---

## What changed in the method, and why

The governing principle, ruled by the operator: **no record or claim stands on a source that is not
credibly independent of what it establishes.** It asks a different question from the source ladder.
The ladder grades the *artefact* — is this the document, or somebody's account of it. This grades
the *relationship* between the artefact and the claim it is made to carry. A ministry press release
is a good source for what the ministry said and no evidence at all that the ministry succeeded.

Five rules follow.

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| **R1** | `worked` requires evidence independent of the announcing body. Where none exists the record is **not** `failed` — the outcome is *unestablished*, not negative, so it takes `partly` or `contested`. |
| **R1a** | Intra-state evidence — one arm of the state measuring another — satisfies the principle **where the measuring institution published the figure as part of its own statutory or routine function**, and fails where the figure appears **in support of the claim**. The test is on the publication, not the institution. The source must bear on the limb in dispute, and the document must be **held**. |
| **R2** | Where a commitment states several objectives and **any one is unmeasured**, `worked` is unavailable and the verdict is `partly`. No centrepiece exception. |
| **R3** | A stated, quantified commitment with no deadline takes `undated-commitment`, not `no-objective`. |
| **R4** | A foreign national government primary takes `T1F`, its own tier. |

And the rule that made the pass a single operation: **every rule is evaluated against one snapshot,
and the corpus is written once.** That is not tidiness. The qualifying intra-state sources for
L-0023, L-0026 and L-0029 were four `abclive.in` citations, and band A re-tiered exactly those
citations T1 → T4 in the same pass. Applied in sequence, **the order would have decided three
verdicts**: re-tier first and all three fail independence on a tier the pass was about to withdraw;
test first and all three pass on a tier that was about to be wrong. Neither answer is about the
evidence. `tools/pass-2026-08-06.mjs` reads the corpus once, evaluates every rule against that
snapshot, and writes once.

outcome nobody independent measured is unestablished, not negative. A rule that turned missing
evidence into a negative finding would be the same error as one that turned a press release into a
positive finding, pointed the other way.

## The searches that ran before the rescoring

Independent evidence was searched for **before** any record was moved, so that records would be
scored on evidence rather than on its absence. Two searches landed and they point in opposite
directions, which is the test that the standard is not an instrument for producing a preferred
answer.

**L-0207 — found.** *Punatsangchhu-II Hydroelectric Project Achieves Full Commissioning*, Ministry
of Energy & Natural Resources, Royal Government of Bhutan, 27 August 2025. Bhutan is a
**counterparty, not a bystander**, and its ministry is independent of the Indian ministry whose
claim is under test. Plus BBS, 11 April 2026, for the Nu 5.10 tariff — which satisfies this record's
own revisit trigger. **Not searched, and named as such:** Bhutan's Royal Monetary Authority annual
report, the National Statistics Bureau trade series, the DGPC annual report.

**L-0047 — found, and it cuts against the record.** CAG Report No. 6 of 2024, retrieved as a PDF in
full and the figures read from it. A constitutional auditor reporting to Parliament on its own
initiative is the clearest qualifying case R1a has.

**L-0053 — found, and it supports the record.** CPPI 2025, retrieved and the Indian rows read from
the ranking table. It corroborates the direction on a basis independent of the ministry, though not
the specific figure: the index measures container vessels, the record's turnaround figures are
all-cargo.

**The RBI *Financial Stability Report* — not retrieved by this client, and not "not published".** It
was the stated prerequisite of the pass, because it is the document that would let L-0026 and
L-0029 pass R1a on one limb. Six routes were tried and named on each series that depends on it:
`rbidocs.rbi.org.in` refused the TLS handshake from two independent stacks; a second fetching agent
reported the socket closed; the in-app browser was denied the host; `www.rbi.org.in`'s report
listing returned HTTP 200 at 65 KB and is a JavaScript shell serving no document; the Internet
Archive availability API returned HTTP 429 twice; the Archive replay returned a bot-protection
challenge, which was not answered. **The report is published and this instrument does not hold it.**
That changes no verdict here — R2 bars `worked` on both records independently — but it is the
difference between a test passed and a test not reached, and the records say which.

## The three records that left the ledger

**A self-audit does not belong in the scored ledger.** The ladder grades external documents by their
distance from the event, and a derivation over the corpus's own `/data` is at no distance — it is
the corpus restating itself. A source is not independent of what it establishes when it **is** what
it establishes. So L-0218, L-0219 and L-0220 left, and the ledger went from 226 records to 223.

**Their findings are preserved and are now more checkable than they were.** All of it is at
[/derivations](https://india-government.vercel.app/derivations/), generated from public `/data` with
the rule printed beside every number:

- **L-0220's** thirteen handover-counted schemes and two controls, with the discriminator stated as
  a rule a reader can apply to any record rather than as the record's conclusion.
- **L-0219's** bare-root partition — 288 occurrences, 277 record-url pairs, 93 roots — and its
  246 citations naming no document.
- **L-0218's** boundary, named in section 3: its channels finding is a retrieval result throughout.

**What a reader loses, and it is not nothing.** Section 5 of that page now says so in the same
place: the `caseFor` and `caseAgainst` — L-0220 argued against itself that measuring at the handover
is not laziness, and a table of thirteen ids does not carry that; the declared absences; the
caveats, including L-0219's record of three occasions when its own method misled it; the revisit
triggers; and a place in the verdict-by-domain and verdict-by-tier cross-tabs. **A reader comparing
the corpus over time will see three ledger records disappear, and none of them was withdrawn on its
merits.**

**What is not lost is the arithmetic** — the half that was never checkable, because it rested on a
private repository returning HTTP 404 to every reader.

| Band | Citations | Move | What it is |

| **A** | 10 | T1 → **T4** | Journalism and relayed accounts tagged as Indian official: `business-standard.com` ×3, `images.assettype.com` ×3, and four series reaching the RBI *Financial Stability Report* through `abclive.in` |
| **B** | 4 | T1 → **T2** | Multilateral tagged as Indian official: `treaties.un.org`, `comtradeapi.un.org` ×2, `data.worldbank.org` |
| **D** | 19 | T1 → **T1F** | Foreign national government primaries: `govinfo.gov` ×13, `supremecourt.gov` ×3, `whitehouse.gov` ×2, `ustr.gov` ×1 |
| **E** | 65+ | **stays T1** | Archived copies of Indian official documents. Identical bytes are the document, and of 17 archived primaries tested only 2 still answered at their original host — grading mirrors down would tell a reader the evidence is weak when the publisher had deleted it. |
| **C** | 2 | **gone** | The self-citations left with their records. Band C never had a home on the ladder and inventing one would have been the wrong repair. |

**Band B was not in the operator's enumeration and was moved anyway.** UN Comtrade and the World
Bank are multilateral by the ladder's own text, the mis-tier was already in the decision set, and
leaving four known-wrong tags standing would have contradicted the instruction that the set moves
together. It is flagged here rather than folded in silently.

**T1: 965 → 928.**

| | citations |

| less band D | −19 |

Two figures were projected before the pass: **946** (band D alone) and **929** (all bands). The
actual is **928**, one below the projection, and the difference is worth stating rather than
rounding away: the projection assumed L-0218's two citations would be *re-tiered*, one to T2 and one
staying T1. Instead both left the ledger with the record. The projection was right about the bands
and wrong about the mechanism for one citation.

`/method` **derives** this figure rather than stating it — `citations()` and `tierCounts()` in
`lib/data.ts` count it at build time, so the page could not have carried a stale number through this
pass. That accessor exists because the same paragraph once said *"752 are graded T1"* while the data
said 965.

## The withdrawn-wording backfill

This instrument's convention is that a correction quotes what it withdrew, in the same field. **It
was described as the instrument's practice while being true of 6 fields out of 30** — that framing
was mine and it was wrong.

It is now a stated requirement, and the backlog was closed from git in this pass:

| | fields |
|---|---:|
| carried a correction marker before the pass | 30 |
| departed with L-0218 | −1 |
| already quoted the withdrawn wording | 5 |
| **recovered from git and backfilled** | **23** |
| **exempted by name** | **1** |
| unrecoverable | **0** |

The exception is **L-0011's `assessmentNote`, and it is not a gap.** The field did not exist before
the correction: the record carried a failure verdict with no stated reasoning and the correction
*created* the note. Nothing was withdrawn, so nothing is quoted, and the field now says which of the
two it is. Without that exception written down first, a backfill comes up exactly one short and the
gap reads as an oversight.

One ordering detail, because it nearly went wrong: the exception has to be tested **before** the
already-quotes test. L-0011's note does contain a quoted span — it quotes the verdict vocabulary it
is reasoning about — so a quote test run first files it as compliant and the explanation never gets
written. A proxy for *"quotes the withdrawn wording"* cannot tell one quotation from another.

**The gate follows the convention and is still not built.** Built before this backfill it would have
tested 6 fields and passed 24 by having nothing to look at, which is the shape of a control that
reports green because it is looking at an empty set.

## What this pass does not settle

- **`undated-commitment` has no members yet.** L-0209 is the certain case and L-0213, L-0187 and
  L-0210 are candidates; assigning them is a separate judgement about four records and was not part
  of the standard's application.
- **The RBI *Financial Stability Report* is still not held**, so L-0026 and L-0029 rest on relayed
  accounts of the document that would qualify them.
- **The independent review has not been run.** No part of this has been checked by anyone who did
  not write it, which is stated on `/method` and remains true of the pass itself.
