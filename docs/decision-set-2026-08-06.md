# The decision set — everything that moves when the set moves

Standing at `6a403d9` plus this batch. **Nothing here is applied.** This is the list the single
re-scoring and re-tiering pass will work from, grouped by the ruling that moves it, with the verdict
or tier each record would take and the reason stated beside it.

Five rulings are in force, all 2026-08-06: the **governing principle** (no record stands on a source
not credibly independent of what it establishes), **R1** (independence required for `worked`), **R1a**
(the intra-state test), **R2** (an unmeasured limb prevents `worked`), **R3** (`undated-commitment`),
**R4** (`T1F`).

### L-0047 Railway electrification — **found, and it cuts against the record**

- **FOUND.** **CAG Report No. 6 of 2024, Union Government (Railways), Performance Audit —
  *Energy Management in Train Operations and Renewable Energy Initiatives in Indian Railways*, for
  the year ended March 2022**, signed 19 July 2024. PDF retrieved in full and the figures read from
  it rather than from a search snippet. A constitutional auditor reporting to Parliament on its own
  initiative: the paradigm **qualifying** case under R1a.
  - 51,261.02 of 66,066.44 RKM electrified to March 2022; 25,375.40 RKM (38.41 %) during 2017-22.
  - **Shortfalls against the annual electrification target in every year of 2017-18 to 2021-22, of
    16.60, 46.94, 59.11, 21.65 and 24.75 per cent.** Eight zones short by over 1,000 RKM.
  - *"delays in every stage of project planning to project execution"*.
  - The 2.8 billion litres/year and **342 million tonnes/year CO₂** figures are what Indian Railways
    **envisaged**, quoted by the CAG as the target's premise. **A projection is not a measurement**,
    so **L-0047's emissions limb stays unmeasured** and R2 still bites.
- **What it does not do.** The audit ends at March 2022 and the near-complete endpoint came later, so
  it does not refute delivery. It refutes *"delivered close to fully"* as a description of the
  **schedule**, on the government's own target, measured by the government's own auditor.

## E. What publishing the derivations fixed, and what it did not

`/derivations` is now a page on the public site, generated from `/data` by
`tools/gen-derivations.mjs` and rendered from the generated file, and both L-0219 and L-0220 cite it
instead of a private repository that returned 404.

**Fixed:** L-0220 entirely — the thirteen and the two controls are recomputed with the discriminator
stated as a rule a reader can apply. L-0219's first two partitions — 288 bare-root occurrences / 277
record-url pairs / 93 roots, and 246 citations naming no document — same.

**Not fixed, and it cannot be fixed by publication:**

- **L-0219's 139 unaddressable citations** (54 refused / 50 under 500 characters of text / 23 without
  a DNS record / 12 forbidden).
- **L-0218's three unreadable channels.**

Both are **retrieval results**, and no retrieval is stored in this repository. Re-running the check
answers a different question — what the hosts do today, not what they did when the record was
written. Under the governing principle **a claim no reader can check does not stand**, so the options
are:

1. **Store a dated sweep.** Have `tools/source-response-check.mjs` write a timestamped result file
   into `/data` or `docs/`, and re-cite both records to it. Makes the claim checkable *as a record of
   what one client saw on one day*, which is what it always was — L-0219's own caveat says so. Cheap,
   and the honest fix.
2. **Reword to the tool.** Drop the counts and state only that the tool exists and what it measures.
   Loses the finding.
3. **Withdraw the counts.** Treat them as unsourced and remove them. Loses the finding and the
   record's reason for existing.

**Option 1 is the recommendation**, and it is a data change rather than a schema change, so it is
inside the sizing of a later batch. Until one of the three happens, both records assert numbers no
reader can verify, and that should be said on their faces rather than left for a reviewer to find
again.

## F. The withdrawn-wording convention — measured, and it is largely aspirational

I have described this convention as one of the instrument's strengths more than once. **It is not
yet one.** 30 fields across 24 records carry a correction marker and **6 quote the wording they
withdrew.** The other 24 assert that something was corrected without saying what it said.

**Backfilling is cheap, because git has almost all of it.** Every correction-marked field was walked
back through the commit history of its own data file and the last differing value extracted:

| | fields |
|---|---:|
| carry a correction marker | 30 |
| **quote the withdrawn wording today** | **6** |
| **a prior value exists in git** | **29** |
| of those, the prior is itself pre-correction — i.e. **it *is* the withdrawn wording** | **26** |
| prior is itself a corrected version, so the original is one commit further back | 3 |
| **no prior value in git at all** | **1** |

The three double-corrected fields are **L-0114.caveat**, **L-0221.whatHappened** and
**L-0226.whatHappened**; recovering them means walking to the next differing revision, which the
same script does.

**The one that is not recoverable is L-0011.assessmentNote — and it is not a gap in git.** The field
**did not exist** before the correction: the record carried a failure verdict with no stated
reasoning, and the correction *created* the note. There is no withdrawn wording because nothing was
withdrawn. **The convention does not apply to a field that was previously empty**, and that exception
has to be written into the convention rather than discovered by a backfill that comes up one short.

**What backfilling would take:** one pass over 26 fields, each edit inserting the recovered sentence
in quotation marks into the existing correction clause, plus 3 deeper walks, plus one exception
recorded. The archaeology script is written and its output is above. No schema change, no verdict
change.

**And only then the gate.** The check that a correction-marked field contains a quoted span, and that
no sibling prose field still asserts what the span says, is worth building once its subject exists.
Building it today would gate 6 fields and pass 24 by having nothing to test — **which is the shape of
a control that reports green because it is looking at an empty set**, and this instrument has been
caught by that before. **The convention comes first, the gate follows it.**
