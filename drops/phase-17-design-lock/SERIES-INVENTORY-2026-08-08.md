# THE SERIES INVENTORY — 269 series by domain, with spans and declared seams

**Generated 2026-08-08 from `/data` at the commit carrying this file.** Every figure below is
computed from the records, not carried from an earlier note — the deferral-with-a-measured-rate
failure this instrument has already paid for twice. Re-run rather than re-quote.

**Why it exists.** Phase 18 has to choose what the instrument leads with, and the choice has to come
from what the corpus can actually support. The test the operator set is
**would a normal person notice this in their life?** — applied against corpus strength, not against
a list of subjects that sound important.

## THE HEADLINE, AND IT IS UNCOMFORTABLE

**Corpus strength and lived salience are close to INVERSELY correlated in this instrument.**

- **`federalism` is the best-evidenced domain in the corpus — 236 of 236 India observations
  verified, the only domain at 100 per cent** — and almost nothing in it is noticed by anyone.
  *Tamil Nadu's inter-se share of the states' portion of the divisible pool* is a seventeen-point
  verified series running to FY2026-27. No one experiences it.
- **`employment` and `infrastructure` are the two subjects a normal person notices most — work and
  roads, trains, power, housing — and between them they hold ZERO verified India observations.**
  Employment is 76 of 76 `approx`; infrastructure is 94 `approx` and 1 `pending`. Checked against
  the records, not inferred from a status tally: every India point in both domains carries
  `approx`.
- **`banking` is 4 verified of 132.** `welfare` — Ujjwala, PMAY-G, the schemes people are actually
  enrolled in — is **12 verified of 70**.

**So a homepage built from lived salience would lead with the instrument's weakest evidence, and a
homepage built from evidentiary strength would lead with tax devolution.** That is the design
problem stated in its real terms, and it is not solved by picking harder.

**`education` is the one domain strong on both axes** — 54 series, 366 India observations, 309
verified, spans reaching back to 2001, and subject matter a parent meets directly: whether a
Standard III child can read a Standard II text, whether the local school still exists, how many
teachers it has. **If one subject leads, the inventory says it is this one**, and it says so on
strength rather than on taste.

**Reading notes.** `India pts` counts India observations only; the 18 panel series also carry BGD,

`breaks[]`, which is why `education` at 67 and `federalism` at 36 are the two domains no view can

(rule 3), and 50 pending points across the corpus never render without a flag.

## WHAT THIS CONSTRAINS IN THE DESIGN PHASE

1. **Any lead subject drawn from `employment`, `infrastructure`, `banking` or `welfare` leads with
   `approx`.** That is not a reason to avoid them — an honestly flagged approximation is a real
   finding — but a homepage cannot present those numbers the way it can present education or
   federalism, and the flag cannot be quieted to make the page calmer.
2. **`macro` cannot show a single GDP line, ever.** Rule 5: three incompatible bases, all three
   always, in base order, with every seam intact, and overlap years carrying every valid figure.
   Whatever the homepage does with growth, it does with three series and two seams.
3. **182 declared seams across 269 series.** Seams are not an edge case to handle later; they are
   roughly one per one-and-a-half series, and `education` alone has 67.
4. **128 series carry a caveat and rule 3a forbids truncating any of them**, at any density. That is
   the standing design-queue item and this inventory sizes it: nearly half the series layer.
5. **52 series declare an absence, and A-4 says none of those declarations reaches a series listing
   row.** A homepage that lists series inherits that defect unless A-4 is closed first.
