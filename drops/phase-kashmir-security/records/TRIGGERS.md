# TRIGGER B — FIRED (1 of 2)

## B-1 · The 16/5/1/0 civilian residual · `L-0112`, `P-73`, `jk-civilians-killed-composite`

**The figure.** MHA restated its J&K civilian-death figures in the Annual Report 2022-23: 2018 from 39
to 55, 2019 from 39 to 44, 2020 from 37 to 38, 2021 unchanged at 41. The arithmetic residual is
**16, 5, 1, 0**. The footnote that arrived fourteen months later says the new column includes civilians
killed in encounter and counter-terrorism operations. It is therefore extremely tempting to read the
residual as "civilians killed by security forces" — a figure that exists nowhere else in the Indian
record and that this phase would be the first to publish.

**Why it is a trigger B and not a finding.** The basis cannot be established from the sources retrieved,
and two facts positively contradict the reading:

1. **The 2021 residual is zero**, which would assert that no civilian died in any encounter or
   counter-terrorism operation in Jammu and Kashmir in 2021. No document makes that claim and the
   Amshipora case alone makes it implausible.
2. **The security-force-killed figure for 2020 also moved across the same restatement**, from 62 to 63.
   No civilian-definition change can touch that number, so the restatement demonstrably carries ordinary
   revisions as well as the definitional change, and the residual is a mixture of unknown proportions.

**What was done instead.** The two bases are authored as two series with a declared break
(`jk-civilians-killed-terror-basis` and `jk-civilians-killed-composite`), the residual is named and
prohibited in the `caveat` of both `L-0112` and `jk-civilians-killed-composite`, and `P-73`'s
`bridgeNote` records it as a hard stop. **No point was rendered.**

**The judgement needed from the operator.** None on the figure — it must not be published. The judgement
is whether the residual should be carried anywhere at all as a *bounded* statement (for example, "at
most 16 for 2018, of unknown composition"), or whether even a bounded form invites the misreading. This
drop takes the stricter option and states the residual only inside a prohibition.

# TRIGGER A — ASSESSED, NOT FIRED, with a concentration the operator should see

**The question.** Is any `assessment` value used outside its written definition?

**Verdict: no.** Every one of the 15 ledger records satisfies the written text of `contested` — "the
evidence supports more than one defensible reading and the record does not choose between them" — and
each carries a `caseFor` and `caseAgainst` that a capable advocate of each side would recognise.
`reversed` was specifically tested and rejected on seven records where it might have been reached for
(`L-0111`, `L-0112`, `L-0119`, `L-0120`, `L-0121`, `L-0123`, `L-0124`): in each, what ended is a
disclosure, a publication or a reporting practice, and the definition states that withdrawing a
disclosure is not reversal even where the authority withdrawing it is the one that established it. The
Amshipora court-martial outcome was likewise not scored `reversed` — the Armed Forces Tribunal is not
the enacting authority, and judicial action of that kind is outside the value by its own text.

**The concentration that should nonetheless be reported.** **All 15 ledger records are `contested`.** The
field carries no discriminating information for this phase. The cause is structural rather than a
failure of nerve:

- Eight of the fifteen are **institutional or measurement records** — a series restated, a column
  redefined, a table deleted, a stock discontinued, a chapter with no cell, a legislative route
  terminated, an instrument that stopped existing. None is a measure with a stated objective, so
  `worked`, `partly` and `failed` are unavailable by their own definitions ("achieved the objective
  stated at announcement"). `too-early` is the wrong mechanism. `baseline-context` is schema-tied to
  term `baseline`. `reversed` is excluded as above. **`contested` is the only value whose written text
  fits, so it is functioning as a sink for the whole class.**
- The precedent is already set: the education phase reached `contested` for exactly this class
  (`L-0092`, `L-0094`, `L-0096`, `L-0107`), and `L-0094`'s own `assessmentNote` states the reasoning
  this drop follows.
- The other seven are genuinely contested arguments, so they are indistinguishable in the data from the
  eight that are contested only in the sense that the vocabulary has nowhere else to put them.

**The judgement needed.** Whether the enum needs a value for *an established institutional or
measurement finding whose significance admits two readings*. That is a creation question and therefore
the operator's, not mine. It is recorded here and not acted on — this is the same shape as the education
phase's TRIGGER A on `L-0096`, and the two should probably be decided together rather than separately.

# TRIGGER D — APPLICATION LIMB · `defence` · ASSESSED, NOT FIRED

**The question the operator set.** `defence` currently has exactly one user, `L-0009` (the Depsang
incursion), an external border incident, and there only as a *secondary* tag alongside `foreign`. This
phase makes `defence` the primary domain of an entire internal counter-insurgency arc. Is that a
definitional term applied outside its existing usage?

**What this phase actually does to the value.**

| | before | after this drop |
|---|---|---|
| ledger records carrying `defence` | 1 | 10 |
| as *primary* (first-listed / substantive centre) | 0 | 3 (`L-0110`, `L-0117`, `L-0119`) |
| series carrying `defence` | 0 of 186 | 13 |
| provenance carrying `defence` | 0 | 10 |
| subject matter | external, inter-state, one incident | internal counter-insurgency, seven years, five casualty populations |

**The definition's own words:** *"defence: military and security. One record; sparsely attested."*

**My reading, and it is that the words cover it.** "Military" plainly reaches the Army and Rashtriya
Rifles conducting encounters against armed militants. "Security" is the broader of the two words and is
doing work the first does not; it reaches counter-terrorism operations, infiltration across the Line of
Control, and casualties on all sides of an armed conflict. Nothing in the definition restricts it to
external threat, to inter-state matters, or to defence *policy* as against defence *operations*. The
trailing clause — "One record; sparsely attested" — is a statement about the state of attestation, not a
boundary condition; it is the same form of note the enum uses for `demography` ("NEVER USED"), where it
plainly describes observed practice rather than intended scope.

**Where I drew the line, because the words do not stretch everywhere.** Crowd control of protest crowds
is *not* filed under `defence` in this drop. The pellet series and the stone-pelting series carry
`governance`, and `L-0114` and `L-0118` carry `governance` and not `defence`, on the reasoning that
policing a protest is enforcement rather than military action and that reading "security" onto it would
be the extension that genuinely stretches. That line is visible in the numbers above: 13 of 15 series
`defence`, 2 `governance`, and the split is not arbitrary.

**Why I am not manufacturing a stop.** The operator's instruction was explicit that the test is the
definition's own words and that a stop must not be manufactured where the words genuinely cover the use.
They do. I record the magnitude of the extension because first substantive use fixes a boundary here as
much as it does for `kashmir`, and because a reader may reasonably weigh "existing usage" more heavily
than I have — the trigger's own text names usage, and by usage alone this fires. **If the operator's
reading is that observed usage governs, this is a trigger D and the drop should be held.** I have stated
my reading and the evidence for it rather than deciding for them.

# TRIGGER D — CREATION LIMB · NOT ENGAGED

**No new enum value is needed and none was created.** The question was tested seriously, because the
alternative reading of `defence` above would force one (something like `internal-security`). The test
came out negative: with `defence` reading as "military and security" and `governance` reading as
"institutions of state, enforcement, transparency and rights", every record in this drop lands on a
value whose written text covers it, and the two divide the material along a real seam — the conflict and
its casualties on one side, the instruments and the accountability machinery on the other.

The pressure that *does* exist is on `kashmir`, and it is a definitional pressure rather than a coverage
gap: nine records were filed per the written definition where the honest answer is that Kashmir is the
primary subject. That is documented in `KASHMIR-PRIMARY-FLAGS.md` and, per instruction, **not resolved
here.**

**An unusual use of the pairs layer, flagged not fired.** `PR-31` has neither side occupied by a series:
`a` is `competingAccountsFrom` a provenance record and `b` is `absenceFrom` a ledger record. The schema
permits both side types and `kind: contested` fits its own text ("neither answers the other and neither
may be shown alone"), but no existing pair is built this way. Recorded in the pair's own `notes`. If the
operator considers `contested` to require two *instruments*, this pair should be dropped rather than
reworded.

**A citation weakness, not a trigger.** `L-0115` (Amshipora) rests entirely on relayed reporting: the
Court of Inquiry has never been published, the court martial was closed, and the Armed Forces Tribunal

are common ground between the Army, the police, the court martial and the Tribunal — but no figure in it
is primary-retrieved and it should not be upgraded on the strength of the institutions it concerns.
