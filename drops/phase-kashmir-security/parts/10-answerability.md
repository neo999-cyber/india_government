# 10 — ANSWERABILITY: the J&K legislature as a counting instrument, and its removal

This part exists because the operator asked whether the 2018-2024 absence of a sitting J&K assembly affected the answerability of security questions, and flagged that the answer is itself a finding. It is, and the finding is stronger than "there was a gap". **The instrument was not suspended and restored. It was suspended, and then restored in a form that is barred by statute from the subject matter.**

Note on scope: the assembly, statehood restoration and elections are phase-12 subjects. They are referenced here only as they bear on whether security quantities can be asked about and answered. No research part here is devoted to them in their own right.

## 3. What the restored assembly is not permitted to answer — two identifiable refusals

The assembly returned in October 2024. These are the two documented instances I could establish where a security-adjacent question or debate was refused on Reorganisation Act grounds. Both are RELAYED via press; I did not retrieve assembly records.

**Refusal 1 — March 2025, budget session.** At the first budget session in seven years, MLAs were barred from asking questions on "terrorism, law and order, All India Services and related issues which are in the domain of Central Government". Roughly 1,300 questions were submitted.
Source: Kashmir Dot Com, 2 March 2025 — https://kashmirdotcom.in/2025/03/02/jk-assembly-barred-from-home-ais-queries-mlas-to-prioritize-works-power-ic-rdd/
NOT ESTABLISHED: how many questions were actually rejected, and on what individual grounds. The article gives the category, not a count. A count would make this far stronger and does not exist in anything I retrieved.

**Refusal 2 — March 2025, Billawar/Kathua civilian killings.** Speaker Abdul Rahim Rather disallowed a discussion on the killing of three civilians in Billawar, Kathua district, invoking s.32 of the Reorganisation Act, and additionally citing Rule 59 (no debate while a probe is pending) on the ground that the Lieutenant Governor had ordered an investigation. Ruling-party (National Conference) and Congress members had demanded the discussion.
Source: Deccan Herald — https://www.deccanherald.com/india/jammu-and-kashmir/jammu-and-kashmir-assembly-speaker-denies-debate-on-kathua-killings-citing-reorganisation-act-3439793 (direct fetch returned 403; content known through search extraction — treat as RELAYED and thinly so).

**Refusal 3 — 28-29 October 2025, ex-gratia to families of victims of violence.** Speaker Abdul Rahim Rather deleted a question tabled by Langate MLA Sheikh Khursheed (Awami Ittehad Party) asking the reasons for the "inordinate delay" in providing ex-gratia payments and benefits under SRO-43 to the families of victims of violence in J&K, on the ground that the Home Department falls under the Lieutenant Governor's jurisdiction under the Reorganisation Act. The Speaker told legislators generally that they cannot ask questions pertaining to departments under the LG's control.
Sources, all RELAYED, mutually corroborating: The Tribune — https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/speaker-asks-mlas-not-to-put-up-questions-related-to-departments-under-l-g (retrieved, 29 October 2025); Kashmir Observer — https://kashmirobserver.net/2025/10/28/speaker-asks-mlas-not-to-put-up-questions-related-to-departments-under-lg/ ; Daily Excelsior — https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/jk-assembly-speaker-asks-mlas-not-to-put-up-questions-related-to-departments-under-lg/ ; Statetimes; Kashmir Vision.

Refusal 3 is the cleanest of the three for schema purposes. It is a **specifically requested figure, specifically refused, with an identifiable refuser, date, questioner and stated ground.** That is the `withheld` test met exactly, and it is met on a question about compensation to families of victims of violence — a quantity that sits squarely inside the security arc.

## 4. The contested reading of s.46

There is a live disagreement about how far the bar reaches, and both positions are on the record.

- **Muzaffar Iqbal Khan**, Rules Committee member and former judge: "If the LG frames rules...we cannot ask questions on law and order matters and police actions." He argued this prevents scrutiny of police conduct that the cabinet cannot control in any case.
- **Speaker Abdul Rahim Rather**: the LG can only restrict matters requiring his discretionary action, and must consult the Speaker before doing so.

Both RELAYED via The Wire, 5 January 2025 (URL above).

This is a **different-weightings** disagreement, not a different-facts one, and it should be recorded that way. Neither party disputes that s.46 exists, that it empowers rule-making, or that consultation with the Speaker is required. They disagree about the breadth of "matters which affect the discharge of the functions of the Lieutenant Governor". The subsequent conduct — refusals 1 to 3 — is evidence about which reading is operating in practice, and it runs toward the broader one, but a pattern of Speaker's rulings is not the same thing as a settled construction of the section.

---

## 5. What this means for the phase

1. **A `not-published` or `withheld` absence on any J&K security quantity cannot be given "ask in the J&K assembly" as its closing route.** That route does not exist for this subject matter. Any absence route must run to Parliament (where MHA answers) or to an RTI addressee or a court. Stage 3 must not author a J&K-assembly route.
2. **Parliament is now the only legislative route**, and MHA is both the answering ministry and the producer of the figures.
3. **The 2018-2024 period is not a gap in a series that resumes.** It is the termination of an instrument. Series sourced to J&K assembly replies end in 2018 and do not restart.
4. Refusal 3 is a ready-made `withheld` record with a real refusal attached. Refusals 1 and 2 are weaker — refusal 1 has no count and refusal 2 was additionally justified on a pending-probe rule that would apply in any legislature — and should be graded down accordingly rather than stacked as three equivalent instances.

- Whether any security question **was** admitted and answered in the restored assembly. I found refusals; I did not search exhaustively for admissions, and the absence of a found admission is not evidence of none. Do not write "no security question has been answered since 2024" — that is not established.
